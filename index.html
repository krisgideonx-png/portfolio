About

Considered design, built properly.

I'm a web designer and front-end developer. I care about the unglamorous parts of the craft — a clear information hierarchy, a type scale that still holds up at 390px, contrast that actually passes, and motion that respects prefers-reduced-motion.

That means every page starts from a small, deliberate system: a limited palette, a fluid type scale, a 4px spacing rhythm and a handful of reusable components. The result is work that looks considered, reads easily and loads fast — rather than a pile of one-off styles.

The projects below show the range: a full brand homepage rebuild, a written UX audit and redesign proposal, and an editorial site with interactive filtering. Different briefs, same method.

  • Design systemsPalette, type scale, spacing and components defined once, reused everywhere.
  • AccessibilityLandmarks, skip links, focus-visible states, alt text and reduced-motion support.
  • Responsive layoutDesktop, tablet and mobile verified at 1440, 834 and 390 pixels.
  • PerformanceCSS-transform animation, IntersectionObserver reveals, no layout thrash.
  • UX auditHierarchy, contrast, consistency and conversion gaps, written up with fixes.
  • Content structureReal copy and information architecture — never lorem ipsum.
